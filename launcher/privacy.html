Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Inriris LLC · San Juan, Puerto Rico · webmaster@inriris.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Inriris LLC ("Inriris", "we", "us") handles information in connection with SlugLauncher (the "Service"), a web app launcher available at https://git.inriris.com/launcher/.

1. Short version

  • SlugLauncher does not require an account.
  • Your launcher configuration (apps, folders, titles, theme, language) is encoded in the URL you use, not on our servers.
  • When you open a launcher, your browser contacts third-party icon services (Google favicons, the sites you added, and optionally Iconify or our Cloudflare Worker for SVGL) to display icons.
  • We store a few preferences locally in your browser (theme, last hash) and a cache of icons in IndexedDB with a 30-day validity.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising cookies.

2. What SlugLauncher stores, and where

  • In the URL fragment (after #SLUG): apps, folders, names, URLs, icon URLs, page title, language, theme and a "saved" flag. The fragment is part of the link you keep or share; it is not sent to our servers by the browser.
  • In localStorage: slTheme (your last theme choice) and sluglauncher:lastHash (your last launcher, used only to restore an installed app opened without a hash).
  • In IndexedDB (database sluglauncher-icons): a cache of icons you have opened or shared, kept for up to 30 days, so the launcher opens faster. You can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

3. Third-party requests

To show icons, your browser may contact third parties. This is normal web behavior; we do not control those third parties. Please review their own privacy policies.

  • Google favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) and other Google-hosted services when you add or open an app with a Google-provided icon.
  • The websites you add — for example, to fetch their favicon.ico or og:image metadata.
  • Iconify (api.iconify.design) when you use the built-in icon picker and choose Iconify.
  • SVGL (svgl.app) when you use the icon picker and choose SVGL. Requests may be proxied through a Cloudflare Worker operated by Inriris at /launcher/icon-svgl and /launcher/icon-proxy.
  • Cloudflare as the CDN/hosting provider in front of Inriris (it may log technical data such as IP address, time and requested URL, per its own policies).

Opening a link to a third-party icon, page or service may reveal your IP address and technical request data to that third party. This is true of any web browsing.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email or password to use the Service.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not intentionally collect data from children under 13.

5. Server logs and security

Our hosting and CDN providers may automatically log technical data (such as IP address, user agent, timestamp and requested URL) for security, abuse prevention and operational purposes. These logs are processed under those providers' own policies. We use them only to operate and protect the Service.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, do not use the Service. If local law sets a higher minimum age, that higher age applies. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can clear localStorage and IndexedDB from your browser settings to remove local preferences and the icon cache.
  • You can stop using the Service at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, access, correction or deletion). To exercise them, contact webmaster@inriris.com.

8. Data retention

We do not keep a central database of launcher configurations. Local preferences and the icon cache remain in your browser until you clear them or they expire (30 days for icons). Server-side logs at our providers are retained according to those providers' policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the Service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use the Service at your own risk and avoid putting secrets into launcher names, URLs or titles.

10. International users

The Service is operated from Puerto Rico (United States). If you access it from outside the United States, you do so on your own initiative and are responsible for compliance with local law.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be reflected here; continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
